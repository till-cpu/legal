1. Read this part properly

Spearo is a logbook and a planning aid. It is not safety equipment, and it will not help you in an emergency.

Spearfishing and freediving are dangerous. Shallow-water blackout kills experienced divers, gives no warning, and happens near the surface. No app prevents it.

  • Starting a dive in Spearo does not notify anybody. The dive record stores your return time and the buddy you selected so that you have a record. It does not message them, alert them, or raise anything if you do not come back. Tell a real person on shore before you get in the water.
  • Spearo does not monitor you. It records positions only while the app is open and does not watch for anything going wrong.
  • The dive tables, band, weighting and roller calculators are estimates. They are simplified models, not certified dive planning tools. Verify against your training and your own equipment.
  • Never dive alone. Dive with a buddy who watches you on the surface.

You are responsible for your own safety, your training, your equipment and your decisions in the water.
